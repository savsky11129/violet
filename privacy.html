Violet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Violet is a personal budgeting app made by Violet Demo. This policy explains how your information is handled.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you add to Violet (bank statements, transactions, budgets, goals, prices, reminders and settings) is stored only on the device where you use Violet, and is encrypted with a passcode you choose. Violet has no servers and no user accounts, and the app is blocked from connecting to the internet. Your data is never sent to us, and we cannot see, access, recover or share it.

Your passcode

Your passcode is not stored anywhere, not even on your device, and it is never sent anywhere. Only you know it. If you forget it, your data cannot be recovered by you, by us or by anyone else. The only option is to erase Violet’s data and start again.

Bank statements

Statements you upload are read and processed on your device. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

Where the app comes from

The web version of Violet is delivered by GitHub Pages, a website hosting service. When you install Violet, or open it while connected to the internet, your browser may download the latest version of the app’s code from GitHub Pages. These requests never include your data. Like any website, GitHub may see technical details such as your device’s internet address and the time of the request; GitHub’s own privacy statement covers that.

Reminders

Statement and price-check reminders, and the calendar file you can save, are created on your device. Reminder messages don’t include amounts or other financial details.

No tracking, no ads, no selling

Violet does not use analytics, advertising, tracking tools, or crash reporting that sends data off your device. We never sell, rent or share your information with anyone.

Your control

You can export your data at any time from Settings → Export my data. Exported files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe. You can permanently delete all of your data from Settings → Delete all my data, or by removing the app and clearing your browser’s data for it. Because we hold no copy, deleted data cannot be recovered by us.

Backups

Violet does not make backups or send your data to any backup service. The iPhone and Android app versions of Violet are set to leave your data out of iCloud and Google backups. For the web version, your phone, computer or browser may include website data in its own backups; any such copy stays encrypted and cannot be opened without your passcode. To keep your own copy, use Export my data.

Children

Violet is not intended for children under 18.

Your rights

If you are in South Africa, the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives you rights over your personal information. Because Violet does not collect your information, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app with a new effective date.
